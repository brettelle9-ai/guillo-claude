II
Care guide

Looking after yours

Knit fabric needs a lighter touch than woven. Keep it simple.

 
Wash cold

Gentle cycle, inside out. Cold water preserves the knit and colour.

Hang dry

Shade only. Never tumble dry. Heat damages knit fibres for good.

 
Store folded

Fold flat, don't hang. Hanging stretches knit at the shoulders.

No iron

Knit doesn't crease. Light steam only if needed. Never press directly.
