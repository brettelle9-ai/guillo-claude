II
Care guide

Looking after them

 
Cold wash

Machine wash cold with like colours. Preserves the stretch, shape and colour.

Hang to dry

No tumble dryer. The twill dries fast and holds its shape.

 
Low iron if needed

Most creases fall out. For a sharper look, press a centre crease down the front of each leg.

What to expect first wear

Fitted from day one. Not tight, just precise. The stretch twill eases into your shape over the first few wears while holding its structure.

A note on sizing

True to size for a tailored fit. Prefer more room through the leg, size up. After the first wash they reset to their original shape.
