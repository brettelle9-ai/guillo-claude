# Email send times and audiences

Working notes for scheduling Guillo campaigns in Klaviyo. All times are Brisbane (AEST, no daylight saving).

## What other brands do

Will tracked 222 emails from brands he follows, by day and three-hour slot.

How to read it:

- **Ignore the overnight columns.** The 12–6am sends are almost certainly US and UK brands scheduling in their own time zone. They don't tell us when Australians read email.
- **Sunday 3–9pm is the busiest slot.** 23 sends, the clear favourite for a weekly brand email.
- **Wednesday and Thursday 6–9pm come next.** 11 sends each, the main midweek evening window.
- **Saturday 9am–3pm is the weekend daytime slot.** 16 sends.
- **Nobody sends 6–9am or around lunch on weekdays.**

## What Guillo has done (Aug–Oct 2026)

Most Guillo sends went out mid-afternoon or after 10pm. The Cup Day race card went at 10pm on a Monday. Almost none landed in the evening windows above.

Audience mattered far more than time. Every send to the engaged segment ("Active site last 30 days, opened email last 30 days, no order in last 20") opened at 63–77%. Sends to ALL EMAILS opened at 32–40% whatever the time. Without the same audience at different times, the data can't yet show a time effect.

## Defaults

1. **Audience first.** Send to an engaged segment, not ALL EMAILS. ALL EMAILS averaged 34.5% opens over Aug–Oct, against about 70% for the engaged segment.
2. **Main weekly email:** Sunday, 6–7pm.
3. **Midweek email:** Wednesday or Thursday, 6–7pm.
4. **Event-led emails:** the evening before the decision point, in one of the windows above. Not at 10pm.
5. **Test it.** To learn Guillo's own best time, send the same email to the same engaged segment at two times, using Klaviyo's send-time A/B test.
